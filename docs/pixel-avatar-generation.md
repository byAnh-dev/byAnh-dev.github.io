# Pixel avatar trial

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the user's supplied aquarium photograph. Saved asset: `public/prototype/anh-pixel-avatar.png`.

The avatar stays pixel art at rest and on hover. The photograph reveal was removed and the compact 80px desktop / 64px mobile layout restored. Desktop rendering was checked in the live prototype.

## Final prompt

Create a single small pixel-art avatar of the person in the reference photograph for a minimal personal portfolio. Reference is identity only. Preserve recognizable long dark hair, rounded dark glasses, face shape, gentle expression and slight three-quarter head angle toward the viewer's right. Head and shoulders only, black shirt. Style: deliberately coarse hand-drawn 8-bit pixel portrait, approximately 32 pixels wide by 40 pixels tall logical grid, enlarged with perfectly sharp square pixels. Limited palette of about 10 flat colors, warm natural skin tones, near-black hair, muted gray shirt highlights. Very simple block clusters and stair-step silhouettes, no smooth edges, no gradients, no realistic photographic texture, no detailed illustration. Light cool-gray solid background with a single small cobalt-blue square accent near upper right, reminiscent of a minimalist pixel avatar placeholder. Portrait centered and fills most of frame, hair and shoulders visible, face readable at 80px display width. No aquarium, fish, text, border, watermark or UI. Output portrait aspect ratio 4:5. This is an entirely pixel-art portrait, never a pixelated-photo filter and never a polished high-resolution cartoon.
