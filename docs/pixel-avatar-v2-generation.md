## Final prompt

Create a single pixel-art avatar from this new portrait photograph for a minimal personal portfolio. Use this photograph as the sole identity reference. Preserve the front-facing pose, recognizable face shape, rounded pale rose-gold glasses, long dark slightly wavy parted hair, calm closed-mouth expression, black top and a small hint of the delicate necklace. Head and shoulders composition, face centered, hair framing the face, top of head fully visible. Hand-drawn retro pixel art with deliberately visible square pixel clusters and stair-step silhouettes, about 48 by 60 logical pixels enlarged with crisp edges. Limited flat palette, warm natural skin tones, near-black and dark brown hair, light cool gray solid background with one small cobalt blue square near upper right. No room background, no photographic texture, no smooth vector illustration, no gradients, no text or watermark. Keep likeness rather than beautifying or exaggerating eyes. Entirely pixel art, designed to read as a small 80px avatar. Portrait aspect ratio 4:5.
